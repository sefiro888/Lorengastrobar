Frequently asked questions

Before you come

Do I need to book?

During the week there's usually room, but at weekends we fill up —especially at lunchtime— and space is limited. We recommend booking via WhatsApp or on OpenTable.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes! We're pet friendly and we'll bring your companion some water.

Do you have a set lunch menu?

Yes, Monday to Friday at lunchtime, combining homemade Paraguayan and Spanish cooking. Ask us about today's menu.

When is there vorí vorí and pira caldo?

At weekends, in limited quantities. If you're coming especially for them, book and let us know.

Do you host celebrations at the restaurant?

Yes: birthdays, company lunches, afterworks… and you can even bring your own cake. Check the events page.

We're waiting for you. Eju!

"Come" in Guaraní. Bring your appetite and, if you like, a friend who doesn't know Paraguay yet.
