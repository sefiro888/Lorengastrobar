What travels best

Arrives as if freshly made

Our favourites for delivery and a tip from Loren to keep them perfect if you don't eat them straight away.

Empanadas

They survive the journey beautifully and are perfect for sharing.

🔥 Tip: 5 minutes in the oven at 180 °C and they're crispy again.

Chipa guazú

Tasty warm and even better the next day.

🔥 Tip: flip it in a frying pan with a little butter.

Sopa paraguaya

Travels whole and is cut into portions at home.

🔥 Tip: 20–30 seconds in the microwave and it's ready.

Milanesas

Huge portions: one is enough for two if you order a starter.

🔥 Tip: in the oven, never the microwave, so it stays crispy.

Lomito sandwich

The Paraguayan street classic, with chips.

🔥 Tip: open it as soon as it arrives so it doesn't go soggy.

Mbejú with cheese

Best eaten soon: the cheese is just right.

🔥 Tip: hot frying pan, 1 minute on each side.

        Frequently asked questions

        All about delivery

        How far do you deliver?

        Delivery is handled by Glovo and Uber Eats, and each app will confirm whether it reaches your address. It usually covers the neighbourhoods around Camp de l'Arpa and Sant Martí. If it doesn't reach you, come and pick it up!

        What time can I order?

        In the apps you can order while the kitchen is open (on Uber Eats, roughly from 10:45 to 22:30). For pick-up, check our opening hours.

        Can I order the weekend specials too?

        Vorí vorí, pira caldo and soyo are made at weekends in limited quantities. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll tell you if there's any left to take away.

        Do you accept Ticket Restaurant?

        Yes, you can pay with Ticket Restaurant® through Uber Eats.

        Do you do large orders for offices or parties?

        Of course! For groups we recommend our catering trays. Check the events and catering page and ask for a no-obligation quote.

        I have allergies or intolerances, what should I do?

        Before ordering, contact us by phone or WhatsApp (602 07 68 28) and we'll tell you about the allergens in each dish. Many Paraguayan classics are made with cassava and corn, without wheat, but in a kitchen that also handles wheat.

        Are the prices the same as in the restaurant?

        The apps may have slightly different prices from the restaurant. Picking up in store saves you the delivery fees.
