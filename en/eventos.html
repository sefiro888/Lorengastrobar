Frequently asked questions

Before you plan it

How far in advance do I need to order?

For small trays, a couple of days is usually enough. For big events, weddings or special dates, the sooner the better: that way we can secure the day and quantities.

Can I bring my own cake?

Yes, you can bring your birthday cake. Let us know when you book and we'll have it ready for the candles.

Do you do set menus for groups?

Yes. We'll suggest a menu with starters to share, mains and dessert to fit your budget per person.

Do I pick up the trays or do you deliver them?

They're usually picked up at the restaurant on Carrer de la Muntanya 115. If you need another option, just ask.

Do you have options for allergies or vegetarians?

Many Paraguayan party bites can be adapted and several classics are made without wheat. Mention it in the planner and we'll advise you.
