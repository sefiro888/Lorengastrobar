552|Frequently asked questions
553|All about <em>delivery</em>
554|How far do you deliver?
555|Delivery is handled by Glovo and Uber Eats, and each app will confirm whether it reaches your address. It usually covers the neighbourhoods around Camp de l'Arpa and Sant Martí. If it doesn't reach you, come and pick it up!
556|What time can I order?
557|In the apps you can order while the kitchen is open (on Uber Eats, roughly from 10:45 to 22:30). For pick-up, check our <a href="visitanos.html">opening hours</a>.
558|Can I order the weekend specials too?
559|Vorí vorí, pira caldo and soyo are made at weekends in limited quantities. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll tell you if there's any left to take away.
560|Do you accept Ticket Restaurant?
561|Yes, you can pay with Ticket Restaurant® through Uber Eats.
562|Do you do large orders for offices or parties?
563|Of course! For groups we recommend our catering trays. Check the <a href="eventos.html">events and catering</a> page and ask for a no-obligation quote.
564|I have allergies or intolerances, what should I do?
565|Before ordering, contact us by phone or WhatsApp (602 07 68 28) and we'll tell you about the allergens in each dish. Many Paraguayan classics are made with cassava and corn, without wheat, but in a kitchen that also handles wheat.
566|Are the prices the same as in the restaurant?
567|The apps may have slightly different prices from the restaurant. Picking up in store saves you the delivery fees.
