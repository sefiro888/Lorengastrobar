31|Lorenza Vera —Loren to everyone— arrived in Spain more than 17 years ago with a suitcase full of recipes. She studied cooking, worked in restaurants and, in the middle of the pandemic, dared to open her own place to fulfil a dream:
32|"To prove that Paraguayan food is just as excellent as any other."<small>— LOREN VERA</small>
33|Today, together with her family —her son José Manuel is her "right hand"— and a team that will even serve you in Guaraní, Loren Restobar is a meeting point for Paraguayans who miss home and for locals discovering it for the first time.
