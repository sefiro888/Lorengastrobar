242|PARAGUAY
243|Recipes are learnt <em>by watching</em>
244|Loren was born in <strong>San José de los Arroyos</strong>, in the heart of Paraguay. There, as in so many Paraguayan homes, cooking was passed down without books: by watching mothers and grandmothers knead chipa, curdle cheese and light the fire for the Sunday barbecue.
245|Those recipes from her parents and grandparents are still, to this day, the foundation of everything that leaves our kitchen.
246|"Our aim is that with one bite we can take our guests all the way to Paraguay."
247|Tray of homemade empanadas, croquettes and chipas
248|MORE THAN 17 YEARS AGO
249|The leap <em>across the ocean</em>
250|Hoping to give her family a better future, Loren emigrated to Spain. At first she had to leave her children in Paraguay; she worked tirelessly and, in just one year, managed to bring them over.
251|She studied cooking, worked in restaurants and learnt the trade from the inside, without ever losing the flavours of home.
252|Opening in the middle of <em>a pandemic</em>
253|While the world was closing, Loren opened. Together with her husband, José Manuel, she chose the name and set up <strong>Loren Gastrobar</strong> on Carrer de la Muntanya: a small place with 19 seats inside and 12 on the terrace, near Clot and Camp de l'Arpa metro stations.
254|Chipa guazú became the flagship of the house, and empanadas, sopa paraguaya and mbejú started travelling all over Barcelona thanks to delivery.
255|"It's hard, but I don't regret it."
256|Table set for a celebration
257|The family <em>grows</em>
258|On 28 October 2022, <strong>Loren Restobar</strong> was born: more tables, a bigger menu and a slightly more refined offer, with new stars such as <em>mandi'ó chyryry</em> and the mixed grill served on a brazier.
259|Today the whole Loren family welcomes you under that name at Carrer de la Muntanya 115, with the same home cooking as ever and a terrace that's full every weekend.
260|"My right hand is my son."

265|Paraguay won on Instagram, the Paraguayan press got behind it and thousands of compatriots voted from all over the world. The chivito went through, but the pride stayed at home.
